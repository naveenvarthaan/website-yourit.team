Sales Development Representative

Internship · Remote · Entry Level

Internship Remote Entry Level

About the Role

We're an early-stage IT services company specializing in database services — helping businesses design, manage, optimize, and secure their data infrastructure. We're building our sales engine from the ground up and looking for a hungry, hands-on Sales Development Representative to help us find and connect with our first wave of clients.

This is a rare chance to work directly with the founding team, shape how we do sales, and grow fast alongside the company. No rigid processes here — just real ownership from day one.

Key Responsibilities

  • Research and identify potential clients that need database services (e.g., companies with growing data needs, outdated systems, or no in-house DBA support)
  • Generate leads through cold calling, email outreach, LinkedIn prospecting, and other creative channels
  • Set up discovery calls and meetings for the founder / sales lead
  • Track and organize all outreach, leads, and follow-ups in Google Sheets
  • Learn our database service offerings (database design, migration, administration, optimization, backup & recovery, security) well enough to explain them clearly to prospects
  • Help test and refine outreach messaging — emails, LinkedIn scripts, cold call openers — your ideas matter here
  • Meet weekly targets for outreach activity and meetings booked
  • Work closely with the founder to understand what's working and adjust approach quickly

Requirements

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Business, IT, Computer Science, or a related field
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills
  • Comfortable making cold calls and handling rejection with a positive attitude
  • Basic understanding of databases / IT services, or a genuine willingness to learn fast — you don't need to be technical, but curiosity helps
  • Organized and self-motivated — you'll manage your own pipeline in Sheets without much hand-holding
  • Comfortable working in a small, fast-moving, figure-it-out-as-you-go environment

What You'll Gain

  • Direct exposure to B2B sales in the IT services space
  • Real ownership of your pipeline and outcomes from day one
  • Hands-on understanding of database services and the clients who need them
  • Mentorship from the founding team
  • A strong resume line with measurable impact

Key Traits We Look For

  • Self-starter who doesn't wait to be told what to do
  • Resilient — you treat rejection as data, not failure
  • Curious about technology and how businesses use it
  • Clear communicator — written and verbal
  • Detail-oriented when it comes to tracking and follow-ups
Important Note

This is an early-stage role where you will be helping shape the sales process itself. Candidates who thrive in ambiguity and want real ownership — not just a task list — will fit best.
