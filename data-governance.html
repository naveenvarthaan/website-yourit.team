Data Governance & Security

Trustworthy data, audit-ready at every tier. Quality frameworks, access control, lineage, compliance programs, and zero-trust security baked into the platform — not bolted on after the fact.

Service Overview

Trust by Design.
Audit-Ready by Default.

In 2026, governance has expanded to cover AI systems, training data provenance, and model lineage — not just traditional data pipelines. The EU AI Act, DORA, and tightening SOC 2 requirements mean that teams without operational governance are facing real regulatory exposure, not just theoretical risk.

We embed governance into the platform — discoverable lineage, enforced access, data contracts as code, and automated AI model provenance. Whether you're working toward SOC 2, EU AI Act readiness, GDPR, or HIPAA, compliance becomes a side effect of running the platform correctly.

EU AI Act Compliant GPAI model governance, training data docs & bias monitoring
Data Contracts as Law Quality SLAs enforced in pipelines — not just documented
Zero-Trust Access JIT elevation, identity-aware policies & column-level security
AI Model Lineage Training data provenance, version tracking & audit artefacts

Data Quality Frameworks

Lineage & Catalog

Compliance Programs
