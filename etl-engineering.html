ETL / ELT Engineering

Pipelines that move, transform, and deliver — reliably. Batch and real-time data flows engineered for scale, observability, schema evolution, and AI-ready downstream consumption.

Service Overview

Data That Arrives
On Time. Every Time.

In 2026, pipelines are expected to be real-time by default, AI-ready by design, and governed by data contracts — not just tested as an afterthought. When pipelines are flaky, every downstream report, model, and AI agent inherits that flakiness. We build pipelines that engineering teams can trust.

From AI-augmented dbt scaffolding to Fabric Real-Time Intelligence event streams, we work across the modern data stack and pick the right pattern for the job — not the trendiest one. Every pipeline ships observable, contract-enforced, and cost-instrumented from day one.

AI-Augmented Pipelines GenAI scaffolding, dbt docs generation & incident triage
Data Contracts First Schema enforcement & quality SLAs across every team boundary
Streaming as Default Fabric RTI, Kafka & Event Hubs — real-time, not batch-first
FinOps-Aware Engineering Cost telemetry & right-sized compute baked in from build

How We Engage

From Source Survey to Production Runway

01

Source & Target Survey

Catalogue every source system, target zone, freshness need, and data-quality expectation. Output: a prioritized pipeline backlog.
