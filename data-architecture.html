Data Architecture

Blueprint your entire data ecosystem. Lakehouse, data mesh, data warehouse, and AI-ready schema models — designed for your business goals, governed by your standards, and built to scale.

Service Overview

Architecture That Lasts
Past the Hype Cycle.

In 2026, the architectural questions have sharpened: is your Gold zone RAG-ready for AI assistants? Are you designing for open table format interoperability as Delta UniForm and Iceberg REST catalogs converge? Are your data products governed by contracts, not org charts? The vocabulary keeps evolving — the goal hasn't changed.

We design architectures around outcomes. Our architects bring opinionated reference designs — Delta lakehouse on Azure, federated mesh patterns on Fabric, multimodal pipelines for AI workloads — and adapt them to your reality: your skills, your data, your constraints.

RAG-Ready Gold Zone AI assistant grounding, vector stores & lineage-tracked retrieval
Open Format Native Delta UniForm & Iceberg REST — interop across every engine
Data Products at Scale Mesh ownership with data contracts & federated governance
Multimodal Architecture Structured, unstructured & vector pipelines in one platform
