BI & Dashboard Services

Boardroom-ready insights powered by Power BI. Trusted semantic models, decision-grade dashboards, paginated reports, embedded analytics, and the governance to keep them all reliable.

Service Overview

Insights Executives
Actually Trust.

In 2026, Power BI has evolved: Direct Lake mode is now the default for Fabric-backed models, Copilot generates AI-driven narrative insights, and natural-language querying lets business users ask questions without writing DAX. But none of that lands unless the semantic layer underneath is governed and trustworthy.

We build Power BI assets that scale — a single certified semantic model, role-level security, Direct Lake speed, and self-service environments where business teams explore safely. Dashboards become a means of communication, not a debate club.

Embedded as Product BI inside your apps — multi-tenant, branded & secure
